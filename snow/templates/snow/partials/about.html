About Us

Get to know Snow Beauty better

Snow Beauty Unisex Salon

Snow Beauty Unisex Salon is a cutting-edge beauty and training center owned by five dedicated entrepreneurs. Officially opening its doors on November 22, 2024, the salon has already made a significant impact in the industry.

Services and Training:
We offer a wide range of beauty services, including haircuts, styling, makeup, nail care, waxing, and threading. Our center also provides training programs for aspiring beauty professionals.

Digitalization Plans:
Snow Beauty is planning to digitalize its services to offer online booking, virtual consultations, and other conveniences.

Our Mission

Snow Beauty Unisex Salon’s mission is to reduce youth unemployment in Kampala and empower 500 youth by 2030 through skill-building in the beauty industry.

Our Vision

To create sustainable employment opportunities by upskilling young people in the beauty industry and making beauty services more accessible to all.
